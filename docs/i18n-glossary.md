## Language specific conventions

- pt-BR: "estado" for status, "escalonado" for escalated, "VFC" for HRV in engine text, no gendered adjectives after inserted nouns (neutral nouns such as "cansaço" instead).
- es: informal "tú" throughout, Latin American neutral wording ("presiona", "monitorear", "signos vitales", "retraso", "pronóstico").
- ja: polite です and ます form for full sentences, full width punctuation, no spaces between numbers, counters, particles and Latin words, 被ばく for radiation and ばく露 for noise.
- de: informal "du" throughout, "Warnung" for both the alert feature and the Warning status, "Über die letzten {span}" to avoid dative plural problems.

## Open questions for a native speaker

- pt-BR: "Aviso" versus "Atenção" ordering for Watch and Warning, "visão de solo", the imperative crew labels, the anatomy label for the neutral groin position.
- es: "tu normal" as a noun, abbreviations (VFC, FC, FR) for non physicians, decimal comma convention for Mexico, "Chequeo" for check in.
- ja: フライトサージャン versus フライトサージェン, 器官系 versus 体のしくみ, whether the long decision support sentence is too long for compact UI.
- de: "Warnung" doubling as alert and status, "Crew Medical Officer" jargon, formal Sie in the guided tour, "Konfidenz".
